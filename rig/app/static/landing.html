Questions

How does it understand what I type?

Mostly with a large word list and rules written for stickers: moods, actions, props, clothes, colours and captions. For the rare sentence it can't place, it can ask a small AI model to choose from the same options. Every sticker is drawn by our animation engine, never by an image generator, so the mascot looks the same every time.

Do I need an account?

Not to try it. You can make and preview as many stickers as you like. A free account lets you download and save them.

What does Pro add?

Every format with no mark, Lottie files, and packs: save up to 30 stickers and download them as one pack for Telegram or WhatsApp.

How do I add a pack to WhatsApp?

WhatsApp adds packs through a sticker app on your phone. The pack zip includes the steps; it also follows WhatsApp's official pack format, so a developer can turn it into an app.

Can my brand have its own mascot?

Yes. We can draw your mascot into the studio so your team makes on-brand stickers the same way. Get in touch.
